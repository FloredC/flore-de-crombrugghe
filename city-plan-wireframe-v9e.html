Artifakt — Tracing Your Way Past the Blank Canvas
Read case study

Jun 2026 — People don't skip making something for someone they love because they're lazy — they freeze at deciding what to make. Artifakt turns a typed keyword into a hand-traced scaffold, filtered through a real artist's style. I researched the freeze, built a two-pass AI pipeline to resolve it, and tested it across four sessions — refining the scaffold and onboarding along the way. Sendability: 4/4.

PitchPivot - Design Reasoning for Business Impact
Read case study

Oct 2026 — Designers lose influence not because their work is weak — but because they can't translate it into business language in the moment. I researched this, built a working prototype in Lovable, tested it with four designers, and refined it based on what broke. Featured on The Future of UX podcast, episode 140.
